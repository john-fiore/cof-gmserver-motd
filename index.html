Welcome!

Welcome to the Cry of Fear Community Garry's Mod server, based on the Cinema gamemode.

Here, you can watch videos & movies with other players/fans, or just hang out.

Make sure to join the Cry of Fear Discord Server for more immediate updates on the server, including any events we might host here.

Press [F3] to open the points shop & inventory, and [TAB] for the Player List.

Thanks for stopping by, and enjoy your time here!

Server Rules

Last Updated: 09/20/2026

  1. Don't be toxic. Treat others with respect and how you wish to be treated.
  2. No Politics AT ALL. We want the sever somewhat peaceful.
  3. No NSFW or Illegal content/behavior.
  4. No Spamming chat or VC.
  5. Respect everyone's privacy. Don't dox others or encourage harrassment.
  6. No RDM. Keep any PvP outside of theaters and mutual.
